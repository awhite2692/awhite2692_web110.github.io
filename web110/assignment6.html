This experience helped me understand that successful online collaboration involves more than simply connecting to a video call. Audio quality, video feeds, screen sharing, and reliable Internet connectivity can all affect the experience for instructors and participants. The activity also reinforced how Internet collaboration tools can reduce the need for people to travel to a physical location while still allowing them to interact and exchange information. I can apply this knowledge in future positions involving multimedia production, technical support, or instructional design. For example, understanding the capabilities of platforms such as Zoom can help me support online training sessions, assist instructors with technical problems, and contribute to the development of effective virtual learning environments. My experience at Sysmex also gives me a practical understanding of the technical requirements involved in producing professional online training sessions. This activity reinforced the importance of Internet collaboration tools in education and business, especially when people need to communicate and work together remotely. FTP and File Transfers: Through this activity, I learned more about File Transfer Protocol (FTP) and how it is used to transfer files between computers over a network. FTP is particularly useful when working with large files that may be difficult to send through email or other communication services. An FTP client provides an interface for connecting to a server, navigating folders, uploading files, and downloading files. I have previous experience using FTP while doing contract work for MaxPreps as a videographer and editor. As part of that work, I recorded and edited highlights of high school sporting events and uploaded the finished video files using Cyberduck, an FTP client. This experience showed me how Internet file-transfer tools can support professional media production workflows. Video files can be large, so having a dedicated method for transferring them to a server is important when delivering completed projects to a client or organization. Using FTP also helped me understand the difference between creating or editing a file locally on my computer and transferring that file to another location where it can be accessed by others. Learning more about FTP provides additional context for the work I performed at MaxPreps and helps me better understand the technology behind the process. I can apply this knowledge in future multimedia, web development, or digital learning environments where files need to be transferred between computers and servers. For example, FTP or related file-transfer methods may be useful when uploading video content, transferring website files, or delivering other large digital assets. I also recognize the importance of using secure transfer methods when handling sensitive information, since traditional FTP does not encrypt credentials or transferred data. This activity reinforced that file-transfer protocols are an important part of how the Internet supports professional workflows, particularly when large files must be moved efficiently between different systems. Intellectual Property and Peer-to-Peer File Sharing: Through this activity, I learned more about intellectual property and the issues involved with sharing copyrighted material over the Internet. Intellectual property includes creative works such as movies, music, photographs, videos, and other types of media. I was already familiar with peer-to-peer file sharing because I have used BitTorrent to download movies and music. I also have personal experience with the consequences of downloading copyrighted material. In the past, AT&T contacted me because copyrighted material had been downloaded through my Internet connection. I was required to complete an online course that explained copyright infringement, why sharing copyrighted material without permission is a problem, and the possible consequences of continuing to do so. I was also warned that if the activity continued, my Internet service could be terminated. That experience gave me a much more personal understanding of intellectual property than simply reading about copyright rules. It showed me that copyright issues involving digital media can have real consequences for the person using the Internet service. The activity also helped me think about the difference between the technology used for file sharing and the content being shared. BitTorrent itself is a technology for transferring files between users, but the material being transferred may still be protected by copyright. This means that being able to find or download a movie, song, or other file does not necessarily mean that I have permission to use or share it. This is especially relevant to me because of my professional background in video production and multimedia. I have worked with movies, music, video footage, photographs, and other creative materials throughout my career, so understanding the importance of respecting other people's creative work is important in my own field. I can apply what I learned by being more careful about where digital content comes from and whether I have permission to download, use, or distribute it. This activity reinforced for me that the Internet makes it very easy to access and share digital content, but that convenience also comes with responsibility for how that content is obtained and used.
