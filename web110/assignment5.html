LinkedIn: Professional Networking and Career Development. Through this activity, I learned more about how LinkedIn can be used as a professional networking platform to establish an online identity, connect with other professionals, and support career development. A LinkedIn profile functions similarly to an online résumé by allowing users to present their work experience, education, skills, accomplishments, and professional interests in one place. I also learned that LinkedIn can be used to search for employment opportunities, research organizations, connect with professionals, and learn about developments within different industries. These features make LinkedIn useful not only for maintaining a professional presence but also for actively participating in the job-search process. I have been utilizing LinkedIn as part of my own job search, including searching for positions and completing job applications. I include my LinkedIn profile on my résumé and provide the profile when completing online applications so potential employers can easily access additional information about my professional background. My profile includes information about my experience in multimedia production, video production, graphic design, technical skills, education, and other professional qualifications. Using LinkedIn in this way has helped me understand the importance of maintaining a consistent professional identity across different job-search materials. My LinkedIn profile provides employers with additional information that may not fit into a traditional résumé and gives them another way to learn about my experience and qualifications. I can also use LinkedIn to connect with professionals and organizations related to the types of positions I am pursuing. As I continue my education at Harper College and develop additional skills in areas such as web design, Internet technologies, and digital media, I can update my profile to reflect those new skills and experiences. This activity reinforced that LinkedIn is more than a social media website; it can be an important part of a professional identity and an active tool for finding employment, applying for positions, and presenting my qualifications to potential employers. Vimeo: Showcasing Multimedia Work and Building a Professional Portfolio. Through this activity, I learned more about how online video platforms can be used to share multimedia content, reach an audience, and support professional promotion. Video platforms provide a way to present work visually rather than relying entirely on written descriptions or lists of skills. This can be particularly useful in creative and technical fields because an example of completed work can demonstrate abilities that may be difficult to communicate through a résumé alone. I also learned that sharing video content online can help creators organize examples of their work in an accessible location and provide potential clients, employers, or collaborators with a convenient way to review their experience. A video portfolio can include different types of projects, allowing viewers to see the creator's range of skills, production experience, and creative approach. The usefulness of a portfolio depends not only on the quality of the work but also on how effectively the projects are selected, presented, and connected to the creator's professional goals. I have applied this concept by creating a Vimeo page to showcase examples of my professional video production work. My background includes multimedia production, television news, and other video projects, and Vimeo gives me a place to present selected examples of that experience. I include the link to my Vimeo portfolio on my résumé so potential employers can watch my work and evaluate my production abilities directly. This provides evidence of my experience beyond the written descriptions of my previous positions and responsibilities. For example, an employer reviewing my application can see examples of my storytelling, editing, and multimedia production rather than having to rely exclusively on my explanation of those skills. I can continue applying what I learned by maintaining my Vimeo portfolio, selecting projects that best demonstrate my abilities, and ensuring that the work I present is relevant to the positions I pursue. As I develop additional skills through my coursework at Harper College, I may also be able to use online video platforms to showcase multimedia projects related to web design, interactive media, or instructional content. Through this activity, I learned that the Social Media Honeycomb Model can be used to compare social media platforms based on seven elements: identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. Facebook and LinkedIn provide strong support for all seven elements. Both platforms allow users to establish an identity, communicate with others, share content, maintain relationships, develop a reputation, and participate in groups. LinkedIn places more emphasis on professional identity, relationships, and reputation because it is designed primarily for professional networking and career development. Instagram supports most of the Honeycomb elements through user profiles, visual content sharing, comments, messaging, followers, relationships, and reputation. However, groups are less emphasized on Instagram than they are on platforms such as Facebook or Reddit. Pinterest places a stronger emphasis on sharing and discovering visual content. It supports identity, presence, and reputation, but conversations and direct relationships are less emphasized compared with other social media platforms. Reddit strongly supports conversations and groups through its communities, or subreddits. Users can also develop identities and reputations through their participation, while posts, links, images, and other content provide opportunities for sharing. However, personal identity is generally less emphasized on Reddit than on platforms such as LinkedIn or Facebook because users often participate using usernames rather than their real names. Snapchat supports identity, conversations, sharing, presence, and personal relationships, but reputation and groups receive less emphasis in comparison with some other platforms. Tumblr and Twitter/X support most of the seven elements through user profiles, posting and sharing content, conversations, followers, relationships, and reputation. However, formal groups and group-based interactions are less emphasized than they are on Facebook or Reddit. YouTube strongly supports identity through channels, sharing through videos, presence through audiences and subscriptions, conversations through comments, relationships through subscriptions and interactions, and reputation through channels and viewer engagement. However, groups are less emphasized on YouTube than they are on Facebook or Reddit. Overall, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Reddit provide strong support for all seven elements of the Honeycomb framework, while the other platforms support most of the elements but place greater or lesser emphasis on specific areas. This activity helped me understand that different social media platforms are designed around different types of communication and interaction, and that the usefulness of a platform depends on what a person or organization wants to accomplish.
