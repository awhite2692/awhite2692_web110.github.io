Learning Summary

This lesson helped me expand my understanding of how graphics and multimedia are incorporated into web pages. I used Adobe Photoshop to create a raster graphic and Adobe Illustrator to create a vector graphic, then exported them in formats suitable for web use. I also learned how to incorporate audio and video using multiple file formats and how to embed a YouTube video using an iframe. While converting the multimedia files, I encountered limitations with VLC and its missing VP8 encoder, so I used Shutter Encoder to successfully create the required video formats. These skills are directly applicable to my professional background in video production, graphic design, and multimedia development, particularly when creating engaging online training materials and educational content.
